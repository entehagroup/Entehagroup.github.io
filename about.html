About Enteha Group

Enteha Group designs and builds custom industrial machines, robots and equipment. At its core are three experienced mechanical engineers, working with a wide network of collaborators — from industrial workshops to specialist engineers in analysis, quality control and consulting. On every project, each part of the work goes to the people best qualified for it, across the whole chain: concept and detailed design, engineering analysis, fabrication and assembly, quality control and commissioning.

Our members' track record includes 80 postal sorting robots built for Iran Post and a third-generation design produced as a 200-unit series, the first knee-prosthesis test rig of its kind in Iran, five industrial 3D printers, and awards including the Alborz Elite Prize and two second places at the Khwarizmi Youth Festival.

Our backgrounds complement each other: machine and robot design, mechatronics and automation; manufacturing, composites and quality control; and mechanism design, control and medical robotics. Between us we hold degrees from four of Iran's leading technical universities.

Our work has been recognised by the Alborz Elite Prize, the Khwarizmi Youth Festival, the Iran Industrial Design Festival and Sharif Science and Technology Park, and published in Scientific Reports and the International Journal of Mechanical Sciences.

Frictional behavior between bone and additively manufactured Ti6Al4V implants is affected by bone density and surface texture under varying loads

A study of friction between bone and titanium (Ti6Al4V) implants made by selective laser melting (SLM). The friction coefficient was measured for different combinations of bone density, implant surface texture and load — results that support the design of custom orthopaedic implants matched to each patient's bone.
