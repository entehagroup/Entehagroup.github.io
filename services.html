Why Enteha?

  • One owner from design to assembly. Most outsourcing trouble starts when design, fabrication and assembly are in different hands and nobody sees the whole machine. At Enteha, the engineer who designs it stays with fabrication and assembly to the end.
  • A core engineering team plus a partner network. Three mechanical engineers from Sharif University of Technology, IUST, K. N. Toosi and Tarbiat Modares University, working with industrial workshops and specialist engineers in analysis, quality control and consulting.
  • A track record you can check. 80 postal sorting robots built for Iran Post, the first knee-prosthesis test rig of its kind in Iran, the Alborz Elite Prize and two Khwarizmi Youth Festival awards, and papers in Scientific Reports and the International Journal of Mechanical Sciences.

Frequently asked questions

What is Enteha Group?

A group of mechanical engineers in Tehran and Isfahan that designs and builds custom industrial machines and robots, automates production lines and manufactures industrial parts to drawing. Its core is three engineers from Sharif University of Technology, IUST, K. N. Toosi and Tarbiat Modares University, working with a network of partner workshops and specialists.

What do you do?

We have three service lines: design and build of special-purpose machines, test rigs and industrial robots; production line automation (Delta and Siemens PLC, HMI, motion control and control cabinets); and industrial parts and structures made to drawing, with quality control before delivery.

Who is behind Enteha?

Hassan Nasiri Khonsari (Technical & Design Director), Alireza Pirhaji (Manufacturing & Quality Lead) and Seyed Mostafa Banihashemi (Project & R&D Lead).

Where are you, and which cities do you work in?

Our office and partner workshops are in Tehran and Isfahan, but we work for clients across Iran.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

The price depends on complexity, size, accuracy and quantity. Quotes are free and usually take one to two weeks. Projects take anything from one or two weeks to about a year; for a simple special-purpose machine, design takes 1–2 months and fabrication another 1–2 months.

Do you take one-off orders?

Yes. We take everything from a single machine or part to series production; the right quantity for a series depends on the job.

How do we get started?

Send us a description of the job using the form on this page, by phone, email or Instagram. Drawings in any format, photos or a hand sketch are enough; if you only have a physical sample, we reverse-engineer the drawings from it.

What support do you give after delivery?

For machines we designed and built ourselves, we fix any faults for two months after delivery. A longer warranty, repairs and upgrades can be written into the contract however the client wants. If the design came from the client and we only did the fabrication, faults caused by the design are fixed for a separate fee.
